أنتِ العجبُ يا تخمينا
anti l-ʿajabu yā takhmīnā
A thousand ways There are thousands ways to cook a fish, or sit on a bench cross-legged. There are thousands ways to drop the ball from 19th floor and wathc it speeding towards the ground and grass below. There are thousands ways to ride a wave at the pristine shores of the Pacific ocean. And many more to draw and apple — or a bee. There are thousands ways to read a book or human's palm that promises tornadoes and storms. Yet there is just one way to so tell you how a thousand times I think about you.
حانَ وقتُ الغداءِ
